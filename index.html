What is ElderKeep?

One group of friends. One place to hang out.

ElderKeep is a desktop app for a circle of friends who want somewhere that belongs to them — talk, share a screen, and keep a conversation running between sessions. Not a public platform you get lost in. Not a group chat that drops the call at eight people.

Who it's for: a friend group, a guild, a few housemates, a small team — anyone who has outgrown a group chat and doesn't want to rent a server or hand their conversations to an ad company.

  • Voice rooms — push-to-talk or open mic, and your game audio ducks while you speak
  • Screen sharing — show the room what you're playing, watching or working on
  • Channels of your own — split the group into rooms and set who runs them
  • Chat that stays put — images, videos and links preview inline, history is there tomorrow
  • Private messages — end-to-end encrypted, readable only by the two of you
  • Files straight across — sent peer to peer, never stored on a server

Maybe later Right now there is one ElderKeep server and everyone is on it. Self-hosting isn't available yet — but if enough people ask for it, it gets built. No promise, no date.

Features

🎤

Voice Rooms

Low-latency voice with push-to-talk or voice activity detection. Game audio auto-ducks while you're speaking. Mouse buttons supported for PTT.

📺

Screen Share

Share your screen to everyone in your voice channel. Control bitrate, FPS, and resolution. System audio capture included.

⚔️

Your Own Channels

Give the group its own space with as many rooms as you want, custom ranks, and a message of the day everyone sees when they log in.

🎮

Game Browser

Find a server to join without leaving the app, across dozens of popular games plus Roblox. Save favourites and jump back into the ones you played last.

🔐

Encrypted Whispers

Private messages are end-to-end encrypted with ECDH P-256 + AES-256-GCM. Only you and the recipient can read them.

🖼️

Rich Media Chat

Images and videos from imgur, YouTube, Twitch, Reddit, and 10+ other hosts preview inline. Click to expand or open in a floating player.

🎨

Themes

Seven colour themes, your own accent colour, chat colours, fonts and layout — set per person and saved to disk, not to someone else's account.
